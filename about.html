About This Tool

PoE2Settings.com was created by a hardcore ARPG player who has spent thousands of hours in Wraeclast — dying to porcupines, ripping in hardcore, and obsessing over every single frame in juiced T16 maps.

After the Path of Exile 2 Early Access launch, I noticed that most "optimization guides" were either too shallow ("just turn down your settings") or buried in 40-minute YouTube videos. I wanted something interactive, precise, and instantly actionable — a tool where you select your hardware and get a tailored production_Config.ini without editing random forum posts.

The generator combines values reported across the community with the settings exposed in the in-game menu. Where a key cannot be confirmed against a real client install, we say so on the page. This is a tool by a player, for players — not a lab.

How We Source and Verify

We would rather be useful than sound authoritative. Here is exactly what this site is based on:

  • Official sources — patch notes, GGG announcements, and the in-game options menu.
  • Community-shared configs — production_Config.ini contents posted by players, which we compare for recurring key names.
  • Player reports — frame-rate and stutter reports from Reddit, Discord, and comments, used as a signal, not as a controlled measurement.

What we do not have is a hardware lab. Numbers marked as illustrative are community-reported, and we label them as such. The most reliable check is your own machine: change one setting in-game, open your config file, and compare the key it writes against the generator output.

If you find a key that is wrong, missing, or renamed after a patch, email us and we will correct the page and note the change date.

Why This Exists

Path of Exile 2 is an incredible game, but its engine demands careful tuning. The difference between a smooth 144 FPS experience and a stuttery mess often comes down to five or six config file values that aren't even exposed in the in-game options menu. This tool bridges that gap.
