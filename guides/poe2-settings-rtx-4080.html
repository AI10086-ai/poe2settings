Best PoE2 Settings for RTX 4080 — 4K 120 FPS Config (0.5.0)

June 2026 · Updated for 0.5.0 Return of the Ancients

The Nvidia RTX 4080 with 16 GB of GDDR6X VRAM is built for high-refresh 4K gaming, and Path of Exile 2 in 0.5.0 can absolutely use every bit of it. Unlike lower-VRAM cards that must compromise on texture quality at 4K, the 4080 can enable Ultra texture quality with a streaming_cache_pool_size of 2048, virtually eliminating texture streaming artifacts. Here is how to lock 120 FPS at 4K.

RTX 4080-Specific In-Game Settings

  • Renderer: Vulkan. At 4K the difference between Vulkan and DX12 narrows on Ada Lovelace, but Vulkan still delivers ~5% better 1% lows. Every frame counts at 4K.
  • Texture Quality: Ultra. With 16 GB VRAM and a 2048 MB cache pool, Ultra textures look stunning at 4K. PoE2's PBR materials and environment details benefit noticeably from the highest texture preset.
  • Streaming Cache Pool: 2048. This is the highest recommended cache value for 4K. It ensures all texture mip levels are resident in VRAM, eliminating pop-in entirely during breach and delirium overlaps.
  • DLSS: Balanced. At 4K, DLSS Balanced renders internally at 2227x1253 (~58% of 4K) and upscales. The image quality is excellent — better than native 1440p — while providing ~50% more FPS than native 4K.
  • Global Illumination: Medium. Ada Lovelace's improved throughput allows Medium GI at 4K without sacrificing the 120 FPS target. The lighting quality improvement over Low is substantial.
  • Nvidia Reflex: On + Boost. Essential for keeping input latency low at 4K. Without Reflex, the higher GPU load at 4K adds ~8-12 ms of latency.

Recommended production_Config.ini

Tested values for the RTX 4080 at 4K 120 FPS. Paste into Documents/My Games/Path of Exile 2/production_Config.ini:

[DISPLAY]
fullscreen=true
resolution=4K
vsync=false

[RENDER]
renderer_type=vulkan
global_illumination=false
shadow_quality=low
lighting_quality=high
reflections=ssr_medium
post_processing=false
texture_streaming=true
streaming_cache_pool_size=2048
render_scaling=100
dynamic_resolution=false
fps_limit=120
antialiasing=dlss_balanced
bloom=true
engine_multithreading=true

[AUDIO]
max_concurrent_voices=128
music_volume=0
ambience_volume=50

[INPUT]
mouse_polling_rate=1000
raw_input=true
