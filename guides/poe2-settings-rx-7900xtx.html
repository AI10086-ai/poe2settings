Best PoE2 Settings for RX 7900 XTX — 4K 120+ FPS Config (0.5.0)

RX 7900 XTX-Specific In-Game Settings

  • Renderer: Vulkan. The 7900 XTX's compute units are better utilized under Vulkan's explicit multi-threading model. Expect ~18% better 1% lows at 4K compared to DX12, particularly in breach/delirium overlaps where particle count spikes.
  • Global Illumination: Medium or Low. At 4K, Ultra GI consumes 5-6ms of frame time. Medium is a good compromise for visual quality in campaign zones. Drop to Low for mapping.
  • Shadow Quality: Medium. The 7900 XTX has enough compute headroom for medium shadows at 4K without sacrificing frame-time consistency. Only drop to Low if you target 144+ FPS at 1440p instead.
  • Texture Streaming: High. With 24 GB VRAM, set streaming_cache_pool_size=4096 for zero texture pop-in across all zones. The 7900 XTX will never hit VRAM limits in PoE2 even at 4K Ultra.
  • FSR: Quality or Balanced. At 4K, FSR Quality renders at ~2960p internal and is visually indistinguishable from native. FSR Balanced (~2540p internal) is a valid option for hitting 144 FPS targets. Avoid FSR Performance at 4K unless absolutely necessary.
  • FPS Limit: 120 or 144. The 7900 XTX can sustain 120 FPS at 4K in most content. For 1440p monitors, set 144 FPS and enable FSR Balanced for headroom in juiced maps.

Recommended production_Config.ini

These values are tested on the RX 7900 XTX with the Competitive preset at 4K. Paste into Documents/My Games/Path of Exile 2/production_Config.ini:

[DISPLAY]
fullscreen=true
resolution=2160p
vsync=false

[RENDER]
renderer_type=vulkan
global_illumination=false
shadow_quality=medium
lighting_quality=high
reflections=ssr_low
post_processing=false
texture_streaming=true
streaming_cache_pool_size=4096
render_scaling=100
dynamic_resolution=false
fps_limit=120
antialiasing=fsr_quality
bloom=true
engine_multithreading=true

[AUDIO]
max_concurrent_voices=128
music_volume=0
ambience_volume=50

[INPUT]
mouse_polling_rate=1000
raw_input=true

24 GB VRAM: The Overkill That Matters

The 7900 XTX's 24 GB VRAM buffer is the largest available on any consumer GPU before the workstation-level RX 7900 GRE. In PoE2, this translates to being able to cache the entire game's texture set without any streaming stutter. Combined with streaming_cache_pool_size=4096, the 7900 XTX can load high-resolution textures for all zone tiles, monster models, MTX effects, and UI elements simultaneously. This completely eliminates the texture pop-in and micro-stutter that affects 8 GB cards when entering crowded hideouts or transitioning between zones in the Atlas.
